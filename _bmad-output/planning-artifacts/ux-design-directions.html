Maximum information per pixel. Compact rows, inline sparklines, coverage bars, and row counts all visible in the ticker list. Inspired by Koyfin and Bloomberg Terminal — designed for scanning hundreds of tickers quickly.

Direction 4: Card Layout

Tickers displayed as cards in a grid instead of table rows. Each card shows the ticker symbol, asset class, date range, and a mini sparkline. More visual but shows fewer tickers per viewport. Good for smaller catalogs or filtered views.
